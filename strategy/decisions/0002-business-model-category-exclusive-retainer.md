# 0002 — Business model: Category-exclusive monthly retainer

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Context
Three viable monetization models surfaced during strategy work:
1. Volume subscriber base ($300-500/mo, 50+ subscribers).
2. Enterprise whales ($250K/yr, 4 clients).
3. Mid-ticket done-for-you ($3-6K/mo, 6-10 clients per metro).

Operator constraints (15-20 hrs/week, async-first, intermittent connectivity, sales-strong rather than marketing-strong) and concentration-risk tolerance determined the right choice.

## Decision
Sell category-exclusive monthly retainers at $3-6K/mo to regional, owner-operated vendors ($5-30M revenue range) that sell to independent restaurants. Two delivery tiers:

- Tier 1 (starting offer): Enriched lead profiles delivered monthly. Buyer handles outreach. Symbiotic — one profile sells to many non-competing buyers with zero per-buyer work.
- Tier 2 (later upsell): Lead profile plus first-touch outreach on the buyer's behalf. Higher revenue per client but per-buyer work scales with each opening. Not the starting offer.

Target client count per metro: 6 non-competing buyer categories.

## Alternatives considered
- Volume model: requires constant marketing to refill churn; incompatible with async lifestyle and weak on the operator's strengths.
- Enterprise whales at $250K/yr: 6-12 month sales cycles, procurement processes, calendar demands incompatible with cruising lifestyle, and concentration risk where one churn equals 25% of revenue.
- Tier 2 as starting offer: breaks the symbiotic leverage that makes the unit economics work.
- Free-then-upsell ("gateway drug") model: rejected. Anchors price at zero, attracts non-buyers, doesn't validate willingness to pay. Paid pilot at reduced rate ($1K first month) is the preferred low-barrier mechanism.

## Consequences
- First-sale timeline: 60-90 days, not 30. Plan runway accordingly.
- Sales motion is direct outreach plus Zoom calls. No paid acquisition, no content marketing engine.
- One opening enriched once is sold to N non-competing buyers. Leverage scales with buyer count, not research effort.
- Revenue ceiling per metro: ~$20-30K/mo at 6 buyers averaging $4K/mo. Growth requires additional metros.
- No buyer can exceed 25% of total revenue. Hard rule.

## Revisit if
- Cannot close 2 vendors at $3K+/mo within 90 days of starting outreach.
- A clear pattern emerges that vendors won't buy Tier 1 alone and require outreach to perceive value.
- A specific buyer category emerges with willingness to pay materially above $6K/mo for Tier 1, indicating undervalued pricing.
